About Us

Our Story

Juniper & Grain started with a simple idea: a neighborhood café where the coffee is great, the bakes are warm, and nobody rushes you out the door.

Our name says it all. Juniper is for the greenery and calm we wanted in every corner, and grain is for the fresh bread, pastries, and oats that fill the café with good smells each morning.

Whether you're grabbing a quick latte before work or settling in with a friend for the afternoon, there's always a spot for you here.

  • 🥐
    Baked fresh Small batches, made every single morning.
  • ☕
    Coffee with care Slow-poured and made just how you like it.
  • 🌿
    Room to stay Plants on every shelf and a seat for everyone.

A Few Things You Might Be Wondering...

Curious about something? We've gathered a few answers to help make your visit a little sweeter!

Do you offer dairy-free milk alternatives? +

Absolutely! We offer several milk alternatives so you can enjoy your favorite coffee just the way you like it. Ask our baristas about today's options!

Are your pastries made fresh every day? +

Yes! Our pastries and baked goods are prepared in small batches every morning to bring you something fresh and delicious.

Do you have vegetarian options? +

We do! Our menu includes a selection of vegetarian-friendly options. Check the menu for dietary tags or ask our team for recommendations.

Can I bring my laptop and study? +

Of course! We love being a neighborhood gathering spot. Find a cozy seat, grab a coffee, and settle in.

Do you offer seasonal drinks? +

Definitely! Our seasonal specials change throughout the year. Stop by to discover what's brewing this month.

How can I contact the café? +

We'd love to hear from you! Give us a call at (555) 555-5555 or stop by during our business hours.
